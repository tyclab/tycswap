# The tycswap mark

tycswap belongs to the tyclab family, whose mascot is the TycStation octopus:
eight arms, all busy, one operator. `logo.svg` is that mark with the tycswap
twist — the two outer arms each hold a login card and the arrows below them
swap the cards. Everything else is inherited from the TycStation favicon so the
two sit next to each other without a seam.

| element | value |
|---|---|
| tile | `#0e1420`, corner radius 14/64 |
| arm and arrow stroke | gradient `#5aa2ff` → `#8b6cf0` (48 %) → `#ff4a3d`, round caps and joins |
| head fill | `#0b1018` |
| eyes | `#b48cf5` |
| the two cards | dark card, one outlined in `#5aa2ff` (the login going live), one in `#ff4a3d` (the login going back to the store) |
| no vendor marks | never the Anthropic or OpenAI logos; the providers are told apart by colour, not by brand |

## Prompts for an illustrated version

Both prompts assume the TycStation banner as the style reference: a cartoon
octopus at its desk at night, glowing lamps, neon wordmark on the wall.

**Mark (app icon, favicon, README header).**

> Flat vector app icon on a dark navy rounded square (#0e1420). A friendly
> cartoon octopus drawn as clean round-capped line art, its eight arms in one
> continuous gradient from sky blue (#5aa2ff) through violet (#8b6cf0) to
> coral red (#ff4a3d), a dark rounded head with two large lavender (#b48cf5)
> oval eyes and a small smile. The two outermost arms each hold up a small
> rounded key card: the left card outlined blue, the right card outlined red.
> Between the lower arms, two horizontal arrows pass each other, one pointing
> right and one pointing left, in the same gradient, meaning "swap". No text,
> no other objects, no vendor logos, centred, generous padding, crisp edges,
> readable at 32 px.

**Banner (repository social image, docs header).**

> Night scene, cartoon style with soft neon glow. The TycStation octopus sits
> at a desk in front of a curved ultrawide monitor. On the screen: a terminal
> with a short table of account rows, one row highlighted. Two of its arms
> are mid-swap, exchanging two glowing key cards, one blue (#5aa2ff) and one
> coral red (#ff4a3d); a third arm rests on a keyboard, a fourth holds a mug.
> Tall tower speakers with warm lamps either side of the desk, white network
> switches standing on edge under it. On the wall behind, a neon wordmark
> reading "tycswap" in the blue-violet-red gradient. Palette: deep navy
> background, lavender highlights, the gradient as the only saturated
> colours. No vendor logos, no other text. Wide format, 1280×640.
